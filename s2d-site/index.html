S2D is a micro model that listens to the caller and returns a decision — route the intent, hand off to a human, block a robocall — in ~0.4 s on a plain CPU. When it isn't sure, it says so, and your speech-to-text chain takes over.

Pick a call.

Drop-in before your STT.

S2D is an HTTP JSON call: audio in, decision out. It is designed to sit in front of the speech-to-text step of a voice stack — Pipecat, LiveKit Agents, an Asterisk dialplan — and short-circuit the turns it is sure about.

1 /

Audio in

The caller's first turn, 16 kHz mono. No text is produced.

2 /

Decide

Encoder + tiny head → one probability per intent, ~0.3–0.5 s on CPU.

3 /

Act or fall through

Above a calibrated bar: act. Below: call your STT + LLM as today. Nothing is lost.

Measured, not promised.

One 6-core CPU. Real calls = MInDS-14 French (PolyAI, real bank customers), report half: 269 calls never used for training.

Everyone else went big.

Speech-to-decision is suddenly a category — built on billion-parameter audio LLMs, on GPUs or in someone's cloud. S2D is the other corner: micro model, CPU-only, self-hosted, with published French numbers on real calls.

Micro, not mega

~88M parameters vs 0.6B–32B. Fits next to your telephony.

CPU, not GPU

No accelerator to buy or rent.

Your servers

Audio never leaves your network.

The trade-off

Fixed intents trained for you — not any question typed at runtime.
