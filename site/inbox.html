Frequently asked questions

Do my emails leave the company?

No. Tri Mail runs on your machine and reads your mailbox the way an email client would. No email is sent to any outside service.

Does it work with my email provider?

Tri Mail works with any mailbox reachable over IMAP, which covers most providers. For Microsoft 365 and Gmail we check the connection together during the audit.

What if it gets it wrong?

When Tri Mail is unsure it files the email in “To check” instead of guessing. It never deletes anything, and for the first days it can run in observe-only mode without moving anything.

What hardware do I need?

A computer or small server running Linux, even a modest one — no graphics card. If you don't have one, I supply a mini-PC at cost.

And after the 30 days?

Tri Mail is yours and keeps working. Maintenance (updates, category adjustments, support) is optional, from €150/month, no commitment.
